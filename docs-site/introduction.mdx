Sculptured is a local workspace for **Codex and Claude Code agents**. Arrange their roles on a board, define how they hand work to one another, and inspect their conversations while they work.

The board remains your main interface. Documentation lives here, separately from the application.

## Design, run, inspect

1. **Structure** is the editable design: planned agents, groups, inputs, and connections.
2. **Live** shows current sessions, observed communication, and managed child relationships.
3. **Visual progress** and transcripts expose the evidence behind an agent's updates.

<Note>Reported completion and independently passing checks are different states. An agent saying “done” does not prove its result is correct.</Note>

## Local execution, shared definitions

Projects, files, private Library groups, transcripts, and Git operations stay on your machine. Community accounts and published group definitions live in a separate PostgreSQL service. Downloading a block never starts an agent.
