title: Your community account
description: One place for your profile, saved blocks, and publications.
---

Open **Community** from the workspace menu or private Library. Set your community service address once, then open your account and sign in.

Community is your account area. Its navigation contains saved blocks, browsing, your publications, and publishing. There is no separate Account page in the workspace.

## Save and use a block

1. Browse shared groups and inspect every job and connection.
2. Save a version to your account. Saves are pinned to that version.
3. Download the group and import it into your private Library.
4. Review the imported roles and select a local folder before starting.

A publisher updating or unpublishing a block does not silently replace a version you saved.

## Publish a group

Export a group from the private Library. Upload it from your community account, choose a title and license, and review the exact definition before publishing.

Machine-specific paths, model overrides, workflow input, and boundary notes are stripped. Prompt text can still contain private information, so review it explicitly. Imported agents use restricted permissions and fresh sessions.

## Manage identity

Your public display name, preferences, saved blocks, and sessions are stored in PostgreSQL. Credentials and MFA/passkeys are managed by the identity provider. Sign out one session or revoke all sessions from your community account.

Local provider credentials and transcripts are not uploaded to Community. See [security boundaries](/security).
